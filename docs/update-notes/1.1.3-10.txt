Decrumb 1.1.3 (build 10)

Fixes update preparation and retry when Install and Relaunch could appear unresponsive. Update recovery now has a time limit, and blocked installs show an explanation and a retry action.

Quit Decrumb now stops cleaning and exits the app. Your Signal connection, settings, queued links and sent-note records are preserved, even if Pause is set to discard queued links. Your Start at login preference is unchanged.

Choose Hide Decrumb to hide the windows while keeping cleaning running. Opening Decrumb again resumes cleaning.
